Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

At Vinci-Cut AI, we take your privacy seriously. This policy describes what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect your email address and basic account information when you register to provide you with our services. We do not store your credit card details; all payments are processed securely by our payment provider, Stripe.

2. User Uploaded Media (Videos & Images)

Your privacy is our priority. Any video footage, screenshots, or media files you upload to Vinci-Cut AI are processed securely by our AI systems. We do not keep your files. All raw footage and generated output files are automatically deleted from our servers shortly after processing is complete.

3. Cookies and Analytics

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in and ensure the application functions correctly. We may also use basic analytics to understand how our tool is being used so we can improve it.

4. Account Deletion (Right to be Forgotten)

You have the right to request the complete deletion of your account and all personal data associated with it. To permanently delete your account, please send an email to the contact address listed below with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We will manually process your request and erase all your data within 30 days.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about your data, or if you wish to request account deletion, please contact us at: vincicutai@outlook.com
